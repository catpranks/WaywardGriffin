# VRR refactor: present on arrival once amdgpu HDMI 2.1 VRR lands (kernel 7.4)

Trigger: amdgpu HDMI 2.1 VRR. Preliminary work landed in Linux 7.2; the owner
expects completion in 7.4 (the HDMI Forum licensing block was resolved). Under
7.2.6 both AMD connectors show an empty `/sys/class/drm/card1-HDMI-A-1/vrr_capable`,
i.e. the property is not attached. When that file reads `1`, do this.

Why: the NVFBC render loop currently aims a fixed 5 ms ahead of the predicted
next vblank (`RENDER_AHEAD_NS`, see k9hved). With adaptive sync there is no
fixed vblank: the display starts a scan when the flip arrives. The deadline
constant disappears and the output follows the source cadence, which also
removes the slow beat that sweeps the rolling latency mean between about 6 and
14 ms today. Expected steady total near the sum of fixed costs, about 4 ms.

What the beat actually is (measured 2026-09-18): the game's cadence is its own
120 fps limiter, not the Nvidia HDMI-0 scanout at 119.88 Hz (vsync is off, so
NVFBC sees frames at the limiter rate). Against the AMD output at 120.00 Hz the
phase drifts about 35 µs per second, i.e. ~35 ppm, one full-frame slip every
~4 minutes. Switching the AMD output to 119.88 to "match the Nvidia scanout"
made it worse: a clean 8.3 s sawtooth, the 1000/1001 beat against the
limiter. Reverted; monitors.lua stays at 120.0.

Steps:

- Hyprland: `misc:vrr = 2` (fullscreen only) in `~/projects/nix-home/modules/desktop/hyprland.nix`; check `hyprctl monitors` reports `vrr: true` with waygriff fullscreen and `directScanoutTo` set.
- Render loop: drop the deadline path (`PresentationDone` timing → `next_draw_ns`), submit on `CaptureReady`, keep the one-pending-presentation gate and the 50 ms maintenance fallback.
- Cursor: `cursor.no_break_fs_vrr` if hardware cursor updates disturb pacing while unconfined.
- Re-measure per `docs/latency.md` (4-minute confined window); update `docs/cross-gpu.md` pacing section and AGENTS.md.

Interim option not worth taking: turning the game's vsync on would lock its
cadence to the Nvidia 119.88 Hz scanout, and the AMD output at 119.88 would then
match to crystal tolerance. That trades the beat for the game's own swapchain
latency and still does not lock phase. VRR is the real fix.
